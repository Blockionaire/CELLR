## What exists

### Five signals, five places (rule 8 of the brief)

| #   | Signal                     | Where it lives                                                                | Written by                                     |
| --- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | The answer was useful      | `sommelier_feedback` (0046), now with the plan's kind, outcome and confidence | a thumb                                        |
| 2   | The bottle was opened      | `sommelier_outcomes` (a row exists)                                           | "I opened it"                                  |
| 3   | It was had with the dish   | `sommelier_outcomes.with_dish`                                                | "With the dish? Yes / No"                      |
| 4   | The two went well together | `sommelier_outcomes.enjoyed` (`yes`, `mixed`, `no`)                           | only someone who said "with the dish: yes"     |

None is derived from another. A thumb writes no outcome and an outcome writes no thumb (SQL test, service test and
browser test each check both directions). "How it went" cannot be said without "with the dish" (refused by the
schema, by the database function, and by a table constraint).

Migration `20261126000049_sommelier_learning.sql` (**draft, applied nowhere**):

- `sommelier_outcomes`: the person's own, readable by them only (row-level security), written only through
  `sommelier_set_outcome`, which checks that the answer is theirs, alive, a plan, and that it **offered this
  bottle**. What the answer was (kind, outcome, confidence) is copied from the kept answer by the database, never
  taken from the client. No question, dish, note or other typed text is stored. Limited to 120 an hour per person.
  Deleted with the account; kept when the conversation expires (as ratings are).
- `sommelier_set_feedback` is replaced by the same function that also copies the plan's codes.
- `admin_sommelier_report(from, to)`: counts only, service role only.

Behind the switch **`sommelier_learning`** (needs `sommelier_engine`; never turned on by `all`): without it there
is no button, no read of the table, and `PUT /api/v1/sommelier/outcomes` is a 404.

On the screen: under a bottle that a kept pairing answer offered, "I opened it"; then "With the dish?"; then, only
after "Yes", "How did the two go together?". Each choice stays visible, can be changed, and is announced as
pressed; "I opened it" again takes everything back.

### Reporting

`/admin/sommelier` (admins only, a real 404 for anyone else): questions and how many the engines answered without
a model; the model's calls, failures, cost and latency for the sommelier; ratings by what the answer was; outcomes
by what the answer was, signal by signal. No person, household, wine or word is in it (checked in SQL and in the
browser).

Product events carry codes and counts only (`plan-metrics.ts`): engine, source, kind, outcome, confidence, numbers
of picks, set-aside wines, styles and friends, the question asked back. Never the question, the dish, a wine, a
price or a name. `sommelier_outcome_said` carries the three signals and nothing else. `docs/METRICS.md` has the
definitions.

### Routing and cost

With the engines on, a pairing, buying, menu, serving, keeping or quantity question makes **zero model calls**
(benchmark: 0 calls over 222 attempted scenarios; browser test: 0 calls). Only a general question about wine still
goes to the model. See `COST_REPORT.md`.

### Caching (D-24)

There is no cache across questions, and none was added. The engines read a household's wines once per question
and keep the reading for that question only (`gather()`); the p95 of their own work is 4.4 ms, against a budget of
50 ms, so a cache would add a way to leak one household's wines to another and remove no cost worth having.
`intelligence-isolation.test.ts` proves the property that a cache would have to keep: three questions from two
households through the same engines each get their own wines, every read happens once per question, and a
conversation state carried to another household cannot bring a bottle with it.

### The duplicate model call (#76)

Not fixed, with reasons (D-25). The issue's own test for fixing it is "only if double sends show up in
`ai_usage`"; that is production data this work did not read. What changed: with the engines on, most questions
make no model call at all, so two identical requests at once cost nothing for them. The remaining exposure is a
general question, bounded by the chat limit as before. The query for the owner is in `COST_REPORT.md`.

## Limitations

- **Nothing is learned yet.** The signals are recorded and reported. No code changes the engines' knowledge, an
  order of wines or a prompt from them. That needs enough outcomes to mean something (a handful proves nothing)
  and a person to judge them; an automatic loop on self-reported outcomes from few people would be worse than
  none. The register says so (`R-SOM-1`).
- Outcomes are what a person says, not what happened: nobody is asked twice, and most people will say nothing.
- Only pairing picks get the outcome buttons; menu courses and "closest" bottles do not.
- "Bottle opened" is not tied to the app's own "drink" action: opening a bottle through the cellar does not
  record an outcome, and saying "I opened it" does not take a bottle out of the cellar. Joining the two is a
  product decision (it would make the sommelier write to the inventory).
- Answers are counted from product events, which a browser with do-not-track does not send: the report's
  "questions" undercount, and it says so on the page.
- The report has no latency for the engines' own work in production (it is not recorded per question; the
  benchmark measures it).
- V1's AI path is still unmeasured (R-04): there is no measured cost per AI question to compare with.
