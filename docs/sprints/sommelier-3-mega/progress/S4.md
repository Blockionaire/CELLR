## What exists

- **A conversation that remembers** (`packages/domain/src/buying/state.ts`): what the person wants (open, buy,
  give, either), the dish, the price ceiling and floor, online or pickup, a colour wish, guests, what was asked
  and what was turned down. Later statements replace earlier ones ("make that €40", "no salmon after all, stew");
  the rest is kept. The state travels with the plan to the screen and back, validated, never trusted.
- **Questions back** (`nextQuestion`): at most one per answer, never one already asked or already answered, never
  one whose answer could change nothing, and never instead of an answer: the advice is given with the question.
- **Buying advice as a style** (`styles.ts`, `recommend.ts`): 60 styles a shop understands, each read by the same
  wine engine and judged by the same pairing engine against the dish, filtered by the budget and the colour wish.
  A style has an estimate of where sound bottles start ("usually from about €9"); it is knowledge of the market
  on a coarse scale, said as such, and never an offer.
- **Real offers only from a source** (`offers.ts`): an `OfferProvider` interface and the checks every offer must
  pass before it may be shown: identity (the words asked for, in the wine's own name), vintage, bottle size,
  price and currency, budget, merchant, an https link to one of the provider's own hosts, a timestamp with its
  zone that is recent (24 h online, 6 h in a store), stock, and for a store: the store's own confirmation.
- **Four levels of claim**, kept apart in the type: style / product / online offer / local availability.

## What does not exist, and why

**No retailer source is connected.** A real one needs an agreement with a merchant or a data owner (a commercial
and legal decision), and none is approved. So the app's provider is `null`, the plan says "I cannot look in shops
yet, so this is a style to look for, not an offer", and nothing in an answer names a product, a price of a
bottle, a shop or stock. The interface, the validation and the screen for offers are built and tested against a
fixture source that exists in tests and the benchmark only. Scraping was not considered: it is not a legitimate
source. Blocker recorded: R-05; owner decision in `RELEASE_PLAN.md`.

Location: nothing reads a location. Without a source the sommelier does not ask for it.

## Review, tests, limitations

The independent review covered S3 and S4 together: `progress/S3.md`. Tests: `state.test.ts` (55),
`offers.test.ts` (41), the conversation and offer cases in `intelligence.test.ts`.

- Alcohol marketing and age rules were not assessed by anyone qualified; the advice names styles, not products,
  and no link or price is shown without a source. To be checked before buying advice is switched on (R-07).
- No affiliate or sponsored ordering exists; styles are ordered by suitability only.
- A vintage is remembered only within the question that names it.
