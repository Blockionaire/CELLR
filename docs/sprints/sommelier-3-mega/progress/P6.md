# Sommelier V1+ — P6: the `menu` part

2026-11-23, a branch from `main` at `b13cf26` (after P2, #123). Plan:
[`../SOMMELIER_CLOSEOUT.md`](../SOMMELIER_CLOSEOUT.md) §6 (P6). No paid model call, no migration, no switch on, no
production change. The part is off unless `CELLR_SOMMELIER` names `menu`.

## 1. What it does

V1's model keeps choosing the wines, in the one call it always made. The part gives it the structure of the question
and checks what comes back.

| Question                                              | What the model is given                                                       | What is shown                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A menu of several courses                             | The courses, numbered; the answer's schema extended with one entry per course | One row per course: the course in the person's words, its wine as a card, the reason in a line |
| A follow-up about that menu                           | The menu again, as context only; V1's own schema                              | V1's ordinary answer                                                                           |
| "The Tignanello or the Pavillon Rouge with the lamb?" | Both wines kept on the list, and the request to choose                        | Both wines as cards, the one the model would open first                                        |
| Anything else                                         | V1's message and schema, byte for byte                                        | V1's answer                                                                                    |

**Never more than one model call.** The schema, and the room for the answer (70 tokens per course, at most 600 more),
are extended only for a new menu.

**Everything per course is checked in code** (`checkMenuAnswer`): a ref that is not on the list the model was shown
gives that course no wine and no reason; entries are matched to courses by number, the first one wins, numbers out of
range are ignored; a reason with money or a shop in it is dropped; a wine poured with two courses is drawn once and
named again in words. A plan that comes back empty or broken is not shown as a menu: the answer is then V1's ordinary
one, never an error.

**Reused from #95:** `parseMenu` (eight courses at most), ported by hand with its tests. `said` is now the course in
the person's own words rather than the raw piece of text. Not taken: `planMenu` and the engines' orchestration (the
model chooses, not the engine), `asksWhichOf` (a comparison is recognised from two named cellar wines instead).

## 4. The independent end review (2026-11-23) and what was fixed

With the part off nothing changed against `main`, and no combination of parts made more than one model call. Fixed
after it:

| Finding                                                                                                                             | Now                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A course word after its dish lost or merged a course ("…and chocolate fondant for dessert", "…en chocolademousse als dessert")      | Postfix course words split the piece before them; ", and …" after a marked menu is a course; "fish and chips" stays one                                    |
| Not a menu, read as one ("steak tonight, then salmon tomorrow", "I had oysters, then I went for a walk")                            | A piece about another day or the past is not a course; with fewer than two courses left it is not a menu                                                   |
| A course whose wine was made up said "Nothing in your cellar really suits this course"                                              | It says "No wine could be chosen for this course" (`unchosen`); a course without a wine shows the model's reason instead of the general sentence, not both |
| Refs written as `[ref]` or bare were shown raw in a reason, and a later step could make a reason longer than a kept answer allows   | Every form of a ref is rendered before the reason is cut to 240 characters; a name is not repeated when it starts with its producer                        |
| "I love Sassicaia or Barolo, but tonight … what should I open?" read as a comparison, and the model's own pick replaced by the pair | The "or" must stand in the clause that asks; the pair are the cards only when the model pointed at one of them (`comparedCards`)                           |
| `compareRequest` took 155 ms on a long question at 3,000 wines                                                                      | About 10 ms                                                                                                                                                |

## 5. Together with P3 (`check`), after #128 was merged (2026-11-24)

`main` with P3 was merged into this branch. With both parts on, a new menu is asked with prompt v7 and the extended
schema in the one call, and every course's reason goes through the same check as the answer's words, as words about
that course's wine: a ref is rendered, a price or a shop removes the reason, a number of bottles the cellar does not
hold removes it, and the course keeps its wine (which was checked against the list already). The cellar is indexed
once per question (`prepareAnswerCheck`), and a reason never outgrows what a kept menu allows. `service.test.ts` has
the two parts together (a menu, a comparison); the browser tests ran with `check` and `menu` live on the same server
(74 sommelier tests, phone and desktop). Register 1.8.0, reviewed 2026-11-24; no structural change against `main`.

## 6. Together with P4 and P5 (`annotate`, `quantities`), after #129 was merged (2026-11-25)

`main` with P4 and P5 was merged into this branch (no force-push). With the parts together:

- a menu's wines carry the app's facts on their cards, like any other answer's;
- the bottles for the people named are counted course by course: each wine's shares are added before rounding, a
  dessert or fortified wine is poured as a small glass, and every wine needs at least one bottle (`mode: "sequence"`);
  any other answer's cards stay alternatives, of which one is opened;
- a menu question with a serving fact or a number of people gets all its blocks in the one call (the menu first, then
  the facts);
- with `check` as well, the answer and each course's reason are checked, and every answer can be kept and drawn again.

Evidence: `service.test.ts` (the menu with `annotate` and `quantities`, with and without `check`); 83 sommelier browser
tests, phone and desktop, with `check`, `annotate`, `quantities` and `menu` live on the same server; this branch's
service with no part on against V1's own service before V1+ (`b13cf26`), 1,008 runs, 0 differences (a scratch
comparison, not kept). Register 1.13.0 (after the Ratings pull requests took 1.9–1.12), reviewed 2026-11-25; no structural change against `main`.

## 7. What is not proven

- **How well the model plans a menu has not been measured.** The gate asks for a labelled menu set run with the
  model (closeout §6); the set is written, the run needs the owner's budget approval (D-51).
- A plain list without course words is a menu only when the person says so ("menu", "each course", "per gang") or
  writes one course per line. "Oysters, lamb and fondant, what should I open?" is answered as one question.
- Words such as "then" and "dan" can still mark courses outside a menu, when no other day or past tense is named.
- "We had planned oysters to start…" loses that course to the past-tense rule; a marked menu whose first course is a
  question of its own ("What with the oysters? Then lamb.") loses it too.
- A new menu's larger answer allowance raises the worst-case cost estimate of that one question above the proposed
  +10% (free estimate: +13% for three courses, +28% for eight; more in a very small cellar). The proposal is the
  owner's to settle (D-50).
- A follow-up finds its menu only within the history the screen sends (about five earlier questions). A later
  question about something else still carries the menu, marked as context only.
- `compareRequest` does not see nicknames, misspellings or grape-only names, and declines when a third wine is
  named or a name fits two wines and no year settles it.
- With `menu` on and `check` off, a course's reason is checked for refs, money and shops only, not for other claims
  about the cellar.
