# Sommelier V1+ — P7: the `fallback` part

2026-11-23, a branch from `main` at `b13cf26` (after P2, #123). Plan:
[`../SOMMELIER_CLOSEOUT.md`](../SOMMELIER_CLOSEOUT.md) §6 (P7), decision D-49. No paid model call, no migration, no
switch on, no production change.

## 1. What it does

With `CELLR_SOMMELIER` naming `fallback`, a question V1 cannot put to its model is answered by the engines' plan
instead of `plain()`. That is the only time the engines are asked:

| Situation                                                                    | Part off (every deployment today) | Part on                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The model answers                                                            | V1's answer                       | V1's answer, unchanged; the engines are not consulted         |
| No key, the person's limit spent, the model down, an answer failed its check | `plain()`                         | The engines' plan, with the same reason shown; else `plain()` |
| A question of fact (how many, where)                                         | Counted facts                     | Counted facts, unchanged                                      |
| An empty cellar                                                              | "Nothing" answer                  | Unchanged                                                     |
| `sommelier_engine` on (benchmark only)                                       | The engines answer first          | Unchanged: the fallback is not asked a second time            |

The fallback declines, and `plain()` answers as before, when: the engines' own limit is spent, they fail or take
longer than 2 s (their own deadline; no general timeout was changed), they decline the question, the plan decided
nothing, the plan is a buying plan, or a pick is not a bottle of the cellar that was just read.

**Never a price.** The engines are built for the fallback without buying advice and without a retailer source, and
every answer is stripped of styles and offers a second time (`fallbackAnswer`).

**One question back is shown.** A plan that only asks for the dish or the occasion is shown without bottles. Measured
against the alternative (`v1-fallback-plain`): five more scenarios pass on the main set (questions that are not about
wine, where `plain()` offers bottles anyway), none fewer, and no scenario about a dish changes.

"Which wine do I have for the oysters?" is a pairing for the fallback, as it is with the engines on; "how many" and
"where" stay questions of fact.

## 3. Free measurement (no model, no key)

`v1-fallback` − `v1-basic` (today's answer without a model), in percentage points, with dish-clustered 95% intervals
(`compare.ts`, `stats.ts`). The engines are deterministic: one run each.

| Set (scenarios / dishes)      | First bottle right       | No poor bottle offered   | Useful answer           |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| main (124 / 100)              | 83 vs 40: +35 [+24, +45] | 85 vs 10: +60 [+51, +70] | 81 vs 9: +58 [+48, +68] |
| holdout-dev (45 / 22)         | 30 vs 20: +22 [+0, +44]  | 41 vs 9: +71 [+57, +84]  | 37 vs 9: +62 [+43, +79] |
| holdout-test, blind (43 / 22) | 26 vs 12: +33 [+14, +51] | 31 vs 10: +49 [+35, +63] | 26 vs 9: +40 [+27, +51] |

Wall clock per answer, three runs on a shared four-core machine, database reads replaced by in-memory stand-ins:
`v1-basic` p95 2.4–4.1 ms, `v1-fallback` p95 3.4–6.4 ms, maximum under 8 ms.

**What this is, and is not.**

- The labels were written by an AI and reviewed by nobody who knows wine (R-01).
- The main set is the one the engines were developed on; holdout-dev has been read since S9. Only holdout-test is
  blind, and it is small (22 dishes). On holdout-dev the first-bottle interval touches 0.
- The closeout's proposed gate for the fallback (§5: better than `plain()` on first bottle and no poor bottle, lower
  bounds above 0) is met on main and holdout-test and **not strictly met on holdout-dev for first bottle** (lower
  bound +0). The thresholds are a proposal, not an owner decision (D-50).
- It is evidence for the owner's decision, not a quality claim. **The part stays off in production until the
  knowledge has been reviewed by a wine professional (D-49).**

## 4. Tests

  a reload; no sideways scroll; accessibility.

## 5. Together with P3 (`check`), after #128 was merged (2026-11-24)

`main` with P3 was merged into this branch. The two parts do not meet in one answer: a model that answers is checked
and the engines are not consulted; a model that is down, limited or absent gives the engines' plan, which carries no
`check` (it has no words of a model). `parts/fallback.test.ts` has both with the two parts on. Register 1.9.0, reviewed
2026-11-24; no structural change against `main`.

## 6. Together with P4 and P5 (`annotate`, `quantities`), after #129 was merged (2026-11-25)

`main` with P4 and P5 was merged into this branch (no force-push). The engines' plan is an answer like any other to
the two parts: its cards carry the app's facts, and a number of people the person named is counted against them. There
are no words of a model to compare with the cards' serving facts. `parts/fallback.test.ts` has the three parts
together; the browser tests ran with `check`, `annotate`, `quantities` and `fallback` live on the same server. Register
1.9.0, reviewed 2026-11-25; no structural change against `main`.

## 7. Open

- The measurement plays each conversation with the engines' state sent back. A conversation whose earlier turns the
  model answered has no state; that case was replayed once by hand (the same five scenarios differ) and is not
  committed.
- After a model failure the engines' reads can add up to 2 s before the plain answer is given instead.
- `isFactQuestion` repeats the service's own test for a pairing in disguise; a change to one must be made to both.
- The end-to-end spec needs the engines, with their database reads, to answer inside the 2 s deadline. It did in the
  full CI run of the integrated build (run 45254808952, all sommelier specs green); on a much slower runner it would
  fall back to the plain answer and the spec would fail, which is the behaviour it is meant to show.
- The independent end review (2026-11-23) found no defect in this part: never more than one model call, the engines'
  own limit and deadline hold, no price is shown, plans are kept and drawn again, and the committed vectors reproduce.
