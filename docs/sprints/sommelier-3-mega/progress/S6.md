## What exists

Each is the same engines and the same inventory, asked another question, behind the switch
`sommelier_experiences` (needs `sommelier_engine`; never turned on by `all`).

| Experience             | Asked like                                                                      | What the plan holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| How to serve a bottle  | "At what temperature should I serve my Chablis?", "Should I decant the Barolo?" | Per owned wine named: temperature, minutes in the fridge, decanting (air a young firm red; pour an old bottle off its sediment and do not air it), the glass, and whether this rests on the wine's own profile or on a rule for its style |
| How long it keeps      | "How long can I keep my Amarone?"                                               | The drinking window the app already shows for the bottle, its phase, and whether the window is known or the style's guess                                                                                                                 |
| What to cook with it   | "What should I cook with my Barolo?"                                            | Dishes from the food knowledge the engines call good with it (two per kind at most), and what goes wrong with it most                                                                                                                     |
| A wine for each course | "Oysters to start, then roast lamb, and for dessert chocolate fondant"          | Per course its own owned wine, or "nothing suits this course"; a wine is poured twice only when nothing else suits as well                                                                                                                |
| Enough bottles         | "8 guests, roast lamb. Do I have enough bottles?"                               | Half a bottle a guest; the free bottles of every wine that suits; how many are short                                                                                                                                                      |
| Which of two           | "Chablis or Barolo with the lamb?"                                              | The named wines judged against the dish (S3)                                                                                                                                                                                              |

Rules of thumb for serving live in `packages/domain/src/experiences/serving.ts`, quantities in `quantity.ts`,
menus, wine-to-food and comparison in `menu.ts`. A wine's own profile overrules a serving rule only when it is
fact about the exact wine; an old bottle is never aired because a profile said so.

A question about a bottle is only answered for a wine the household owns: "my Chablis" without a Chablis in the
cellar is left to the general sommelier, which may not claim the bottle exists.

No transactional purchase exists anywhere.

Every test runs axe (WCAG 2.1 A and AA) on the finished screen and checks that the page does not scroll sideways;
both are clean on phone and desktop. The screenshots were looked at by the engineering agent (pairing on a phone,
the menu at a desk, buying on a phone).

**What the browser run found, and what was done:**

- **A real routing bug.** "8 guests, roast lamb. Do I have enough bottles?" was answered as a count of the cellar
  ("That wine isn't in your cellar…"): the service took it for a question of fact before the engines saw it. The
  orchestrator's own tests could not see this. Fixed (`asksEnoughFor`: guests and a dish in the question make it
  the engines'), with service tests for both directions (a count stays a count).
- **The "Thinking…" line was a list item with `role="status"`**, which takes away its being a list item (axe:
  `list`). Older than this work; fixed by moving the role inside the item.
- The existing specs `sommelier`, `sommelier-memory`, `sommelier-where` and `friends` were run with it: 79 of 80
  passed; `sommelier-memory.spec.ts:553` (thumbs, desktop) failed once on a count read from the database and passed
  8 of 8 when repeated alone. The test is untouched by this work; it is noted as flaky under load, not fixed.

## Limitations

- **Accessibility is machine-checked only**: axe finds what a rule can find. No screen reader was used and no
  person with a disability tested it. The "Thinking…" state itself is not audited (its text pulses, which axe
  reads as low contrast mid-pulse; so do the existing specs' pages).
- **Seen on the buying screen, not changed:** the first answer already shows the styles while it asks for the
  budget, and the second answer repeats them (now within the budget). It is honest, and long on a phone. Whether
  to hold the styles back until the budget is known is a product choice for the owner.
- CI runs the browser tests on pushes to `main` only, so this spec has **not run in CI**; it adds a fourth
  `next start` to that job.
- Gift discovery beyond style advice (S4) and "recommendation cards with actions" beyond the existing bottle card
  are not built.
- A menu is read from "to start", "then", "for dessert" and the like; a bare list ("oysters, lamb, fondant") is
  read as one dish.
- Quantity needs the number of guests and the dish in the question.
