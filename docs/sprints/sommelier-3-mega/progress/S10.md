# S10 — Hybrid sommelier intelligence and generalisation

2026-11-20. Branch `claude/sommelier-3-s10-hybrid`, on top of `claude/sommelier-3-s9-quality` (#100). Nothing was
merged, deployed, migrated or switched on. **No paid model call was made in S10**; the paid comparison waits for
the owner's approval (`HYBRID_EVALUATION.md` §6). No production data was used.

The owner's goal for S10: not fewer model calls, but more reliable advice than V1 with AI, shown on dishes the
engine has not seen; and no activation if that cannot be shown.

## 1. Does the parser know when it has misread a dish?

`scripts/sommelier-eval/self-knowledge.ts` judges the parser's reading of every distinct question with the
benchmark's own food check (class, and weight, fat, acidity, sweetness, heat, umami within one step). A follow-up
that names no dish is judged on the conversation's dish, as the app reads it.

| Signal (main set, 216 questions) | Questions | Wrong    |
| -------------------------------- | --------: | -------- |
| a known dish, read in full       |        62 | 5 (8%)   |
| a known dish, words left unread  |        86 | 1 (1%)   |
| composed from parts, sure        |        17 | 0        |
| composed from parts, not sure    |        51 | 10 (20%) |

On the first holdout half (45 unseen dishes) a known dish with words left unread was wrong 6 times in 15, and a
composed dish that was not sure 4 times in 7: on unseen food "not sure" means more.

**The router** (`routeDishReading`): send a dish to a model when the parser found no dish but there are unread
food words; when unread words remain that are not about the occasion, the people, the budget, the shop, the cellar
or the asking (a fixed, generic list: no dish word in it); or when the parser itself is not sure. A dish read in
full and sure stays with the parser. Designed on main and holdout-dev only; then applied once, unchanged, to the
blind half (totals only):

| Set                 | Wrong readings | Sent on | Wrong ones caught (recall) | Confident but wrong |
| ------------------- | -------------- | ------- | -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| main                | 16 (7%)        | 48%     | 69%                        | 5 (2%)              |
| holdout-dev         | 12 (27%)       | 67%     | 83%                        | 2 (4%)              |
| holdout-test, blind | 12 (28%)       | 72%     | 83%                        | 2 (5%)              |

**Confident but wrong** is 2–5% of questions: a known dish whose template is off (pho, Korean barbecue, mezze,
smoked salmon blinis, salade niçoise, cassoulet). No router sees these, and a model would not be asked; they need
the knowledge corrected by a person, which is part of the expert review (§6).

## 2. A model reads the dish (C)

`packages/domain/src/food/reading.ts`, `apps/web/src/server/ai/prompts/dish-reading.ts`,
`apps/web/src/server/sommelier/dish-reader.ts`; switch `sommelier_dish_ai` (named only; needs the engine).

- **What the model sees:** the dish phrase only (`dishPhrase`): no `@name`, number, price, occasion, people,
  budget or asking words; at most 12 words. Not the question, the conversation, the cellar, a taste or a friend.
  A question that names a friend (now or earlier in the conversation) is never sent, not even its dish.
- **What it answers:** the engine's own dish profile under a strict schema: the dish's class, ingredients,
  techniques and sauces **from closed lists of the knowledge's ids**, the eleven scales (null where it cannot
  judge), tags from a closed list, a confidence. Nothing about wine is asked or used.
- **Checked by code:** ids the knowledge does not have are dropped; a reading that is mostly made up counts as
  not recognised; an answer outside the schema is refused. "Unknown" stays unknown.
- **Merged, not replaced:** everything the parser read in what the person wrote (a sauce, a way of cooking, a side)
  is kept; the model decides what the dish is built on; the structure is composed from the parts by the
  knowledge, and a scale the model states replaces the composed one; the classics come from the knowledge, never
  from the model.
- **Never sure:** a model reading is at most "medium", so an answer built on it never says it is sure.
- **Any failure** (no key, the platform budget, the person's limit, a timeout, an answer outside the schema) gives
  the parser's reading, exactly as without the switch.
- New AI task `dish_reading`: budget mode **closed** (no call when the platform budget cannot be checked or none
  is configured), its own limit (60 an hour per person), usage recorded per call, the advice model.
- Shared readings: `DISH_CACHE.md`.

## 3. A second opinion in a close call (D, an experiment)

`packages/domain/src/pairing-engine/review.ts`, `apps/web/src/server/ai/prompts/pairing-review.ts`,
`apps/web/src/server/sommelier/pairing-reviewer.ts`; switch `sommelier_second_opinion` (named only; needs
`sommelier_dish_ai`). In a close call (the dish was read by a model or is not sure, the best is only
"acceptable", or the first two are within 5 points) a model sees the dish as the engine read it and the engine's
own picks **as styles** (colour, grapes or places, structure; never a producer, a name, an id or anything of the
household) and may put them in another order, leave some out, or say none suits. Code checks the answer against
the refs it was given: the result is always a subset of the engine's picks. It cannot add a wine or raise a
level. Never for a question that names a friend. Task `pairing_review`, budget mode closed, its own limit.

## 4. Fewer, surer bottles (in B, C and D)

`conservativePicks` (`packages/domain/src/pairing-engine/decide.ts`), studied with
`scripts/sommelier-eval/selection-study.ts` on the readable sets:

| Place 2 and 3, main set                   | Picks | Labelled poor |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----: | ------------- |
| a classic or affinity, nothing against it |   113 | 12 (11%)      |
| a classic or affinity, something against  |    39 | 10 (26%)      |
| structure only, nothing against           |    14 | 5 (36%)       |

On holdout-dev the same split: 4% against 40%. The score distance from the first pick did not predict a poor
bottle. Rule: after the first pick, only picks with nothing against them; when the dish is a guess ("low"), only
the first. The outcome and the first pick never change.

## 5. The evaluation

New, without changing a check: quality per bottle and per place, a "useful answer", "answered when something
suited", bottles offered per scenario, and a paired bootstrap (`scripts/sommelier-eval/quality.ts`; in every run's
output and recorded results). Variants A–D and an upper bound, all on the same scenarios:
`HYBRID_EVALUATION.md`. Results recorded in `results/s10-*.json` (the blind half in totals only) and pinned by the
tests.

**The finding that matters most, measured for free:** a perfect reading of every routed dish (the upper bound)
raises "dish read right" on the blind half from 72% to 95% and does not raise "first bottle right" at all (63%
and 63%; holdout-dev 64% and 64%). V1 with AI gets 73% and 85%. What 3.0 lacks against the model is pairing
judgement on unfamiliar food, not reading. Variant C makes 3.0 safer on misread dishes; it is not expected to
beat A. Variant D is the one that could, and it needs the paid run.

**Paid run:** asked for, not done (`HYBRID_EVALUATION.md` §6, with the decision rule fixed in advance).
