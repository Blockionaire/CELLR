## Confirmed problems and fixes

- The V1 long-answer bug (finding 2). One line in `turns.ts`, one constant, one test.
- Benchmark: measures first counted scenarios the engine could not attempt, which made an engine that does nothing
  look safe (no friend data leaked, 68%). Measures now count attempted scenarios only (`score.ts`).

## Unresolved limitations

- V1's AI path is not measured (R-04).
- The benchmark's labels are AI-written and not reviewed by a wine professional (R-01).
- 113 scenarios cannot be attempted by V1 without AI; they become measurable as S3–S6 add the capabilities.
