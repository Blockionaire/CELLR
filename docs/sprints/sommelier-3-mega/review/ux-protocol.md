# User sessions — protocol (three to five real users)

The owner's provisional plan (2026-11-24) asks **three to five real users** to try the whole sommelier journey, on a
phone and on a desktop, while a facilitator watches and notes. This is about whether people can use it and whether
they trust it, not about whether the wine advice is right (the wine professional judges that).

## Before the session

- **Who:** people who drink wine at home and are not involved in building CELLR. Three to five of them.
- **Where:** a Preview deployment with the sommelier parts switched on and a test cellar (never a person's real
  cellar). A phone (about 390 px wide) and a desktop browser.
- **Consent and privacy:** read the note below to the participant and ask for a yes. Give each participant a
  **code** (P1, P2, …). Write no name, e-mail address or other personal data on the forms. Do not record video or
  audio unless the participant agrees separately; no recording goes into the repository.
- **Forms:** one copy of `ux-tasks.csv` and one of `ux-closing.csv` per participant. Every answer column starts empty.

> **Consent note (read aloud):** "We are testing the app, not you. You can stop at any time without giving a reason.
> We note what you do and say, under a code, never your name. Only counts and short anonymous quotes are kept with
> the project; the filled forms stay with the owner and are deleted after the review. Is that all right?"

## During the session (about 30–40 minutes)

Do every task of `ux-tasks.csv` first on the phone, then on the desktop (or the other way round for every second
participant). Say the facilitator's words as written; do not show or explain the screen. Per task note:

- **could they do it?** yes / with help / no ("with help" when you had to point or explain);
- **time** in minutes and seconds;
- **what confused them**, in their words where possible;
- **would they trust it?** 1 (not at all) to 5 (fully), asked right after the task;
- **remarks**.

Task 7 ("when the AI is unavailable") needs the test server switched to "AI unavailable" before it; switch it back
afterwards. Then ask the questions of `ux-closing.csv` and write the answers in the participant's own words.

## Afterwards

- Keep the filled forms outside the repository. **Only aggregated counts** (for example "4 of 5 did task 4 without
  help on the phone", the median trust per task) and short anonymous quotes go into the project's documents.
- Nothing counts as an assessment until the facilitator has signed each form. How many problems are "not acceptable"
  is the owner's decision (`criteria.md`).
