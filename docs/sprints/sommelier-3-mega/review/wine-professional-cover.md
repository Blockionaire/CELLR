# For the wine professional — cover sheet

Thank you for reviewing CELLR's sommelier. The owner's provisional plan (2026-11-24) asks **one independent wine
professional** to review the content. Nothing you see here has been reviewed by a person before; every verdict
column is empty, and nothing counts as reviewed until you sign below.

## What is asked

| #   | Form                                                        | Rows | What you judge                                                                                  |   Estimated time |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------: |
| 1   | `annotation-review.csv` ([how](annotation-instructions.md)) |   50 | The facts under the sommelier's cards: drink now or keep, window, temperature, decanting, glass |        60–90 min |
| 2   | `knowledge-serving.csv` ([how](knowledge-instructions.md))  |   26 | The serving and decanting rules of thumb                                                        |        20–30 min |
| 3   | `knowledge-windows.csv`                                     |   54 | The estimated drinking windows by style                                                         |        30–45 min |
| 4   | `knowledge-pairings.csv`                                    |   60 | The pairing engine's judgements and its reasons                                                 |        45–60 min |
| 5   | `knowledge-fallback.csv`                                    |   20 | The fallback's most disputed answers, and the AI-written labels                                 |        30–40 min |
|     | **Together**                                                |      |                                                                                                 | **about 3–4½ h** |

Use the three words of [criteria.md](criteria.md) (factually wrong, unwarranted certainty, missed nuance). How many
disagreements make a part "not acceptable" is the owner's decision, not yours: just say what you see. When you are
not sure, write "unsure" and why. You may split the work over several sittings.

**Not asked of you:** the user experience (three to five real users do that, `ux-protocol.md`), or writing the
holdout dishes (other people write those, without seeing any result).
