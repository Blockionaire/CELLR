# Sommelier 3.0 — what the brief asked for and what exists

S9, 2026-11-20. The owner's brief, requirement by requirement, against the code on
`claude/sommelier-3-s9-quality`. Nothing here is released: every switch is off, no migration of this work is
applied (see [`RELEASE_PLAN.md`](RELEASE_PLAN.md)).

States: **built** (code, tests and a browser test exist), **partial** (what is missing is named), **not built**,
**BLOCKED** (needs something outside this repository: a licence, a key, a person).

"Built" means built and tested behind a switch that is off. It does not mean the answers are good enough: that is
the benchmark's question ([`progress/S9.md`](progress/S9.md) §2), and the answer there is "not yet".

## 1. The six points the owner asked about in S9

| #   | Asked for                                                           | State                                    | What exists, and what does not                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Several turns and intelligent follow-up questions in buying advice  | **built**                                | The conversation keeps a state (dish, budget, channel, colour, receiver's likes, guests, what was asked, what was turned down). It asks only what is missing, never twice, at most one question per answer; free text and quick replies; budget, dish or channel can change without starting over. **New in S9:** a friend named in a question stays in the follow-ups ("€50", "and with the fish?"), re-authorised in every request. Missing: the questions are a fixed set of six; there is no free-form clarifying question                                                                                               |
| 2   | Buying advice by budget, dish and personal taste                    | **built**, at the level "style"          | For a dish, a taste, exploration, a cellar gap, an occasion, a present, a table of several people: wine styles with a price "usually from about", and the reasons. Never a product, a shop or a price found: see point 6                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 3   | Shared taste profiles of friends                                    | **built**                                | `@name` with autocomplete; the database says who that is and what they allow, in every request; the friend's own consent switch (off by default); the table's taste orders the suitable wines and never rescues an unsuitable one. Up to five friends per question. Not kept, never sent to a model                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 4   | Recommendations from a friend's shared cellar                       | **built in S9** (was "never used", D-15) | "@thomas is bringing a bottle. What does he own that suits both of us?" judges his wines against the dish and shows them as his. Only with an accepted friendship, his cellar shown to me under the existing sharing rules, **and** a second consent switch of his own that is off by default (migration 0053). Producer, wine, vintage, colour and origin only: never how many, where, his ratings or notes. One friend's cellar per question; carried into a follow-up only when it leans on that question ("and with the fish?"). Without consent the answer says it cannot see his cellar and nothing about what he owns |
| 5   | A present the friend does not already have in the cellar they share | **built in S9**                          | "A present for @thomas that he does not already own": styles that suit his taste, minus the styles found among the wines he shares; the answer names what was left out and says "not found in what they share", never "they do not have it". Compared by style and appellation, not by exact bottle (there is no product to compare: point 6). **Limit:** a friend's wine the engine cannot place (an unknown name with no appellation) excludes nothing                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 6   | Availability online and in shops nearby                             | **BLOCKED**                              | No retailer source with a licence exists (R-05). Built and off: the provider interface, validation of an offer (identity, vintage, size, price, currency, merchant, address, time seen, online or shop), the four claim levels, the location consent question, and the honest fallback on screen ("I cannot look in shops yet, so this is a style to look for, not an offer"). Not built: any provider, the level "concrete product", distance or opening hours. Needs: a contract or feed, then the alcohol marketing and age check (R-07)                                                                                  |

## 3. What changed in S9

Built: the friend's cellar (point 4), presents not already owned (point 5), friends kept across follow-ups (point 1).
Corrected in this document: A-502 in the acceptance matrix said "a friend's cellar is never used"; that was a
decision (D-15), not a completed requirement, and S9 replaces it with consent (D-29).

Still not built, and not marked done anywhere: model wording, free comparison of
two wines, any live offer.

## 4. S11

No new product functionality is on. Built behind an off switch: variant E's guard in shadow
(`sommelier_v1_guard`), which counts what it would flag on V1's own answers and changes none. Proposed, not built:
the engines as V1's fallback, deterministic facts on V1's chosen bottle, a budget and price check for V1's buying
advice (`V1PLUS_ARCHITECTURE.md`).
