# WI-04 — Koopcheck with what CELLR already knows; research once · PASS (behind `purchase_intel`, off by default)

Date: 2026-11-13 · Base: WI-03 checkpoint (`cd44c9c`)

## 1. What a person gets (with `purchase_intel` on)

In a shop, for a wine that is exactly a CELLR catalogue wine and that they do **not** own:

- The comparison no longer stops at "weinig gegevens" because grapes and appellation were left open: CELLR fills
  only the open fields, from the curated catalogue first, then from its own profile.
- A new block, "Wat CELLR al van deze wijn weet": the profile of **exactly this vintage** when a valid one exists
  (style, summary, estimated drinking window), named as CELLR's AI estimate, with its date and the number of sources
  it consulted.
- For a vintage without a profile: the grapes "uit het profiel van 2019", and a sentence saying that a drinking
  window, price or harvest description of another year is never carried over.
- Each filled-in value says where it came from. What they typed themselves is never changed.

No model is asked, nothing is stored, nothing is added to the cellar.

Independently of the flag, two people opening the profile of the same catalogue vintage for the first time at the
same moment no longer both pay for the research (§3).

## 5. Independent review

Done after this checkpoint's first commit (`354ce1b`); recorded in [`WI-04-review.md`](WI-04-review.md). No P1. One
P2 in this sub-sprint: the research claim was taken before the rate limit and never given back, so a failed or
rate-limited first request made others wait for nothing. Fixed in the follow-up commit, with the three P3 and four
minor findings. The rows of §4 about the claim describe the fixed behaviour: a claim is always given back, a waiting
request takes over at once, and the wait is bounded by one research timeout (60 s).

## 7. Not done, on purpose

- The optional AI explanation (brief §4): no demonstrated value over the deterministic reasons, and a new paid path.
- Structure (body, tannin, acidity, sweetness) is available in `knowledge.profile` but is not shown as scales and
  not scored against taste. Scoring it would change `checkPurchase` without evidence.
- Telemetry of reuse (`vintage_profile` / `stable_facts` / `catalog_only` counts) comes with WI-06.
- Koopcheck from a **photo** gets the same knowledge when the confirmed wine is a catalogue identity; that path has
  unit coverage only (the e2e uses the search path).
