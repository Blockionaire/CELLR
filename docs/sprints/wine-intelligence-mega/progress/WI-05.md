# WI-05 — "Bronnen & betrouwbaarheid" on the wine page and in Koopcheck · PASS (behind `sources_panel`, off by default)

Date: 2026-11-13 · Base: WI-04 + review fixes (`11b7569`)

## 1. What a person gets (with `sources_panel` on)

One folded section low on a wine's page, **Bronnen & betrouwbaarheid / Sources & reliability**. Closed it is one
line. Opened it sorts what the page shows into at most four groups, each left out when it has nothing to say:

| Group             | Says                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wijnidentificatie | "CELLR-catalogus (gecontroleerd)" with the pages CELLR's check rests on, **or** "Door jullie ingevoerd" with the sentence that CELLR did not check it and no external source was used |
| Wijnkenmerken     | which filled-in facts (colour, country, region, appellation) share that origin                                                                                                        |
| CELLR AI-analyse  | the profile, the drinking window and the value, each as an **estimate** with its date and the pages it consulted; a window from the rough style table is called that                  |
| Jouw gegevens     | what the household recorded: bottles, opened bottles, purchase prices, tasting notes, photos, an own window or value; "alleen zichtbaar voor jullie huishouden"                       |

An **external source** appears under identification only as "Ook opgenomen in …", with the credit line its licence
asks for, and behind "Licentie en details": the licence and its link, the record at the source, the dataset version
and retrieval date, what CELLR changed (only when it changed something), the sentence that the source is not
affiliated with and does not endorse CELLR, and that a source listing a wine does not make its details verified.

In a Koopcheck result, "Bron van de wijngegevens" opens the same identification block for the catalogue wine.

No source is cleared for people today, so on real data the external part shows nothing. It is exercised with
CELLR's own fictitious test sources.

## 3. Decisions

1. **A verified link is corroboration, not origin.** A catalogue wine's facts stay "CELLR-catalogus" when a source
   also lists the wine; the source is credited as listing it. CELLR's catalogue entries are written and checked by
   the owner, not copied from a provider, so attributing fields to a provider would be false.
2. **"Gecontroleerd" is only ever said of CELLR's own catalogue check.** The credit of a source says the opposite in
   so many words.
3. **Whether a wine is a catalogue wine is the server's statement.** The first version let the page decide and called
   a catalogue wine "door jullie ingevoerd"; the e2e test caught it before the checkpoint (§5).
4. **Nothing of a source is loaded**: no logo, image, frame or script. Links open outside the app with
   `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"`.
5. **Folded by default**, below the history: it must never push the wine itself down the page.

## 5. Defect found by my own test, fixed before this checkpoint

The wine page's data carries no "is a catalogue wine" flag, so the section labelled every wine "Door jullie
ingevoerd" and showed no check pages or credits. Unit tests passed because they supplied the flag. The server now
states it (`WineSourcesData.catalog`), and the model ignores any page-side guess.

## 7. Open point for the owner (rights, not code): R-27

A wine a person creates **from an external record** in the search (WI-02) stores nothing about that record
(ADR-001, decision 7). Its page therefore says "Door jullie ingevoerd" and credits no source. That is accurate about
who confirmed it, but a licence such as CC BY 4.0 may require the credit wherever the adapted data is shown.
**Before any source is switched to `public`, decide** whether to keep the record's id on such a wine so the credit
can follow it. No source is public today, so nothing is shown without credit now. Recorded in `EPIC.md` (R-27) and
`RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md` is the place to tick it.
