**Subject:** LWIN database — licence scope for use in a wine-cellar app

Dear Liv-ex team,

I am the founder of JP Digital Ventures in the Netherlands. We build CELLR, a wine-cellar app for private wine
drinkers: people record the bottles they own and get help finding and understanding their wines. I would like to use
the LWIN database as a reference catalogue of wine identities, and I want to be certain I do so within your terms
before I download anything.

Your LWIN pages describe the database as free to download under a Creative Commons licence, while your website terms
of use (clauses 11.3 and 11.4) and membership terms (clause 9) restrict storing, redistributing and AI-related use of
Liv-ex data. I could not find which of these governs the LWIN download, so I would be grateful if you could confirm
the following in writing.

What we would use: the LWIN-7 code and the descriptive text of each entry (producer, wine, country, region,
sub-region, colour, type, designation, classification). **Not** prices, bids, offers, trades, indices, scores, your
matching tool or any market-data API.

1. Which licence, exactly (name and version), applies to the downloadable LWIN database, who is the licensor, and
   does it cover the whole file including the descriptive text?
2. For that file, does the Creative Commons licence prevail over the website terms (11.3, 11.4) and the membership
   terms (clause 9)? We are not a Liv-ex member.
3. May we store the file in our own application database and show LWIN codes and the descriptive text to our
   (consumer) users, with attribution?
4. May we combine LWIN entries with our own catalogue and other licensed sources, indicating our changes?
5. May LWIN text be included as context when an AI model answers an individual user's question about their own wine
   (inference only; we do not train or fine-tune any model on LWIN data)?
6. What attribution wording and link do you require, and may we name "LWIN" and "Liv-ex" in that credit? We would
   state clearly that Liv-ex is not affiliated with and does not endorse CELLR.
7. How may we obtain updates (frequency, method, and whether each download needs a new registration)?
8. Could you point us to the documentation of the file's columns, of how a wine without a single vintage is coded in
   LWIN-11, and of how deleted or merged codes are marked?

If any of these uses is not permitted under the open licence, I would appreciate knowing whether a separate
agreement is possible and under what conditions.

Thank you for your time.
