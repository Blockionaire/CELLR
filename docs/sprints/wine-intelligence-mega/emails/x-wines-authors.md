**Subject:** X-Wines dataset — question about the origin of the wine data

Dear Dr. de Azambuja, dear co-authors,

I am the founder of JP Digital Ventures in the Netherlands. We build CELLR, a wine-cellar app for private wine
drinkers. Thank you for publishing X-Wines: we have evaluated the wines file (`XWines_Full_100K_wines.csv`) locally,
as a reference list of wine identities, and it is very useful. We cite your article wherever we describe that
evaluation.

Before considering any use beyond a local evaluation, I want to understand the data's origin, because the CC0
dedication covers your own rights and says (section 4(c)) that rights of other persons are not cleared by it. We
would only ever use identity fields — wine name, winery, type, country, region, grapes — never ratings, users or
label images.

1. From which websites were the wine attributes collected, and by what method?
2. Did you check those sites' terms of use, or obtain permission? Are you aware of any contract or database right
   that would restrict commercial reuse of the identity fields?
3. Does the CC0 dedication apply to every version (Test, Slim, Full)?
4. Is the citation of your article a condition of use or a request? We are happy to cite it in the app's sources
   page either way; I would like to word it as you prefer.
5. Is any attribute text copied verbatim from a source site, rather than normalised by you?
6. Do you keep a list of source attributions, and is there a contact for correction or removal requests?

I fully understand if some of this cannot be answered. Whatever you can share will help us decide responsibly.
